## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **VERDICT (lead): this is a TIMING/CLOCK paper, NOT a native 3-state device.** The "frequency" and "phase" in the title are a *clock* — ϕ = −arctan(ωτ) synchronizes the gate drive to the intrinsic relaxation time τ. Frequency/phase adds a **timing degree of freedom** (when the memory forms and decays), not a **third logic level**. There is no push/pull/null device here; the memory effect is bistable (loop exists/collapses; two directions), not ternary.
- **The one genuinely ternary-shaped feature — ambipolar transport — is background, not contribution, and its "null" is fake.** Electron / Dirac-point / hole *looks like* push/null/pull, but the Dirac "null" is a *finite residual conductance* (σ_res regularizes the V-shape minimum, Eq. 3), a continuous minimum, not a cost-~0 open state; the paper assumes a symmetric case (n17) and never exploits the three branches as logic states. It is the textbook "native 3-state" flattery — a continuous V-curve, not a discrete trit.
- **The paper's "polarity" is binary, not ternary.** η's sign gives *two* opposite loop directions (n8); there is no third direction. The floating-gate memory is explicitly *bistable* polarization (n13) — two states, not three.
- **One threshold, not two: this paper sits on the "binary" side of Tau's compute thesis.** The only threshold anywhere is the single V_th in the depletion capacitance (n14). A single threshold is *binary* switching; Tau's "2-threshold tax" — the reason ternary loses on compute — is entirely absent because the paper does no ternary compute at all. It is about memory/sensing/neuromorphic pulse integration, never logic.
- **"Intrinsic recovery" (τ) is a decay, not a state.** τ restores equilibrium after the drive is removed — that is *relaxation toward null*, not the *cost of holding* a null. Reading τ as evidence for a "free null trit" would invert the physics: the null here is the equilibrium the system falls *back to*, not a persistent 0-state it maintains while ± states are cheap.
- **Frequency-locking is orthogonal to the trit state space.** "Frequency as a clock" means the response's *memory window* is tuned by drive frequency (optimal T ~ 2πτ), i.e. it answers "how fast / when", not "which of three values". The DOF it adds is time, and time is a clock axis that composes with any N-state logic, not a substitute for the third value.

## 5. One-liner

Graphene GFET hysteresis is a **timing/clock** paper — the phase ϕ = −arctan(ωτ) is a clock that synchronizes the gate drive to the intrinsic relaxation time τ, so frequency/phase adds a timing degree of freedom, not a third logic level; the only ternary-shaped feature (ambipolar electron/Dirac/hole) is a finite-residue continuous V-curve the paper never exploits as a trit, and its "polarity" (η sign) and memory (bistable FG) are both binary.
