## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **VERDICT: this is NOT a native 3-state transistor, and it is not even native-floor flattery — it is a binary all-optical threshold switch with no electronic ternary path.** It claims nothing about 3 states, so it fails the hunt on arrival rather than flattering it. It is orthogonal to the Tau ternary question.
- **The device is deliberately tuned to kill the third state.** They explicitly set detuning to "avoid any hysteresis cycle (bistable regime)" — the one regime where a third, mid-level state (a latch/memory point) would physically exist — collapsing to a single-threshold "optical discriminator". Ternary needs three levels; this paper's central design choice reduces the device to exactly two.
- **There is no "pull".** The Control only *injects* polaritons (repulsive interaction blueshifts Address into resonance); it never *removes* population. Sign is positive-only: push or null, never pull. The −1 trit has no physical realization here.
- **The one genuinely signed intrinsic DOF is disabled.** Polariton spin (pseudospin σ+/σ−, a native ±1) is the single degree of freedom that maps cleanly onto push/pull polarity — and the paper itself flags it as "advantageous in future devices [38]" then immediately excludes it with linear polarization. The ternary-relevant path exists in the same material system but is explicitly not taken.
- **Information is carried by scalar magnitude, not sign or direction.** On/off is encoded by emission *intensity* (density) crossing a threshold — a level, not a polarity. "Polarity encodes the sign of the trit" is structurally absent.
- **The "free null" is just binary's zero, not a ternary null.** The off state costs nothing to hold, but so does any binary 0. A middle null that is distinct from both push and pull does not exist; the "null" here is only "below threshold".
- **Energy/speed numbers don't transfer.** 1 fJ / 10 ps is at 10 K, per optical switch, and is not comparable to Tau's 0.05 vs 1.20 pJ *wire transport per trit* figure. No per-cell area is measured, so there is no density claim to flatter or refute.
- **The 19× "gain" is fan-out, not density.** It is an amplification ratio (fan-out), not an information-density or area advantage over SLC binary — so it neither validates nor refutes the 0.63× native-floor thesis; it lives outside that comparison entirely.
- **Net:** not the hunt target (no native ternary transistor), not the trap (no "0.63× native floor" claim to debunk) — it is a binary optical switch whose strong suit (cascadability/fan-out) speaks to the *transport/interconnect* half of the Tau thesis and nothing to the *compute* half.

## 5. One-liner

An all-optical binary threshold switch that demonstrates clean cascadability and fan-out with polariton fluids but carries information as a scalar intensity level with no sign, no third state, and a deliberately suppressed mid-level regime — so it offers neither a native 3-state transistor for Tau nor even a native-floor density claim to debunk, only an (unused) native ±1 spin degree of freedom that is the paper's own flagged but untaken path to polarity encoding.
