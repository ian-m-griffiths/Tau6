## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **VERDICT: not a native 3-state transistor. The two polar faces (N-face vs Ga-face) are two *different 2-state device hosts*, not a polarity-encoded third state.** The paper's own framing is "dualtronics" = heterogeneous integration (one photonic device + one electronic device on two faces of one wafer). Every active device here is an ordinary single-threshold 2-state device; no third level is ever claimed.
- **Reversal of the "polarity encodes sign" hope:** "Polarity" in this paper is a *crystal-face label* (which atom terminates the surface — Ga vs N), a static material property fixed at growth, NOT a runtime signal sign. Mapping it onto the trit sign is a terminological collision, not a mechanism (come-to-terms failure). Flipping the wafer is a *fabrication* step, not a togglable state.
- **The null is not free.** The HEMT off-state is standard gate pinch-off (V_GS < V_T ≈ −3 V), costing the same drive energy as any silicon FET. Nothing about the polar physics makes "nothing energized" cost ~0 on the wire.
- **No 2-threshold-tax avoidance.** Single-threshold n-channel FET; the recurring "native 3-state = 0.63× floor flattery" trap doesn't even arise here because no third level is claimed — this is below even that bar.
- **Even the one honest sign-like degree of freedom — electron (2DEG) vs hole (2DHG) carrier polarity — lives on *opposite faces*, i.e. two physically separate devices, and the 2DHG is cited (ref 14) but not fabricated or measured in this work.** Not a single cell that toggles between push and pull.
- **The single genuine touchpoint to Tau's null is the complementary n+p push-pull output stage (N12):** with both devices off, the output is high-impedance "nothing energized" = Tau's null (00). But this is generic tri-state CMOS, available in silicon, and is *not* a property native to the polar wafer. (OURS.)

## 5. One-liner

This paper demonstrates heterogeneous *integration* — an LED on one face and a HEMT on the other face of a single polar GaN wafer — an impressive two-device packaging result whose "polarity" is a static crystal-face label rather than a switchable trit sign, and which therefore offers Tau no native 3-state transistor, no free null, and no escape from the 2-threshold tax.
