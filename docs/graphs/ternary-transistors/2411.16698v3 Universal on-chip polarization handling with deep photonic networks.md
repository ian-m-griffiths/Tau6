## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **VERDICT (lead): this is photonic polarization routing, not electronic ternary — it yields NO native 3-state device useful to Tau. The nearest it gets is an ANALOGY (polarization splitter ≈ push/pull routing), and even that is a binary 1-of-2 demux, not a ternary sign.** Electronic ternary compute gets nothing here.
- **Counter-to "native 3-state":** polarization is a **2-state** degree of freedom (TE/TM), and the routing fabric is 1-in/2-out — every physical axis in this paper is binary (two modes × two ports). There is no intrinsic push/pull/null triplet.
- **Counter-to "free null":** the 50/50 power splitter is **not** a free/null-cost state — it actively throws −3 dB to each of two ports (insertion loss + power division). The "null = 00" reading is OURS, not the paper's physics; the paper has no notion of a ~0-cost null trit on the wire.
- **Reversal on "polarity":** the lens's *polarity* = trit sign (push/pull); the paper's *polarization* = optical TE/TM. This is a terminological collision, not a mapping — "come to terms first" fails if we let the shared word "polar" do the flattering.
- **Counter-to the "0.63× native floor" trap:** none of the three devices claim a native 3-state device; what they claim is *reconfigurability* and *sub-minute design speed*. That is a property of the **design method**, orthogonal to whether the device is ternary-native. Do not read "general purpose" as "3-state native."
- **Reversal on "universal":** the paper's "universal" means "one architecture realizes arbitrary polarization transfer functions" — it is NOT the 3ⁿ vs 2ⁿ addressing advantage of the Tau thesis. Conflating them would be exactly the flattery this survey exists to catch.
- **Counter-to the compute-tax framing:** this device is **continuous analog** routing (phase is a real parameter), the opposite of the discrete, 2-threshold ternary compute where the lens says ternary loses. No thresholding, no 3-level quantization, no Eisenstein-lattice structure anywhere.
- **The one honest rhyme worth recording:** a "no-light" (dark) state *could* be a ~0-energy null on a waveguide — but the paper neither uses nor claims amplitude-as-a-ternary-state, so this is SPECULATION on our side, not the paper's device physics.

## 5. One-liner

A silicon-photonics *design framework* that trains cascaded Mach-Zehnder interferometers to route TE/TM polarization arbitrarily (splitter, 50/50, arbitrary splitter) with >20 dB extinction and 120 nm bandwidth in under a minute — a photonic **ANALOGY** to push/pull routing at best, with **no native 3-state (push/pull/null) electronic device** and no free null, hence irrelevant to electronic ternary compute.
