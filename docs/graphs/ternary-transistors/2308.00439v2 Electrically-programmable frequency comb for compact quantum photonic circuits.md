## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **LEAD VERDICT — PHOTONIC / IRRELEVANT TO TAU.** This is not a transistor, and not a 3-state device. It is a plasmonic four-wave-mixing frequency comb: a quantum-emitter-loaded metal nanostructure in a photonic cavity, voltage-tuned through a Fano resonance to continuously dial entanglement magnitude. There is no push/pull/null, no sign encoding, and no addressing/transport story. It does not even enter the "native 3-state transistor" race — it is a different species of device entirely.
- **"Electrically-programmable" ≠ ternary programmable.** The paper's "programming" is an *analog, continuous* knob: voltage → meV QE-resonance shift → continuous FWM/entanglement degree (L25, L392–394). Tau's programming is *discrete* (12-trit words, 2 bits/trit). The term collides; the semantics do not map.
- **The emitter is explicitly TWO-LEVEL, not three-level.** The QE is written with density matrices ρee, ρeg, ρge over |e⟩ and |g⟩ (L265). The fundamental quantum object is a qubit, not a qutrit — the opposite of what we hunt.
- **"Off / enhanced" is not a free null or a pull.** FWM suppression at ΩQE=ω3 and enhancement at 1.8005ω1 (L107–109) are two points on a *continuous* curve, and both are *magnitude* modulations of an always-non-negative quantity (FWM intensity, log-neg). There is no sign (no −1 direction), so no "pull"; and reaching "off" costs active tuning to an exact resonance, so no free null either.
- **"Transistor" appears only as a speed ceiling, not as the device.** The only transistor references are the 5–100 GHz clock-speed limitation (L31–32, L105–106) — an external constraint on modulation, not a device that the paper proposes or exploits. Counter to any hope of a native transistor here.
- **"Polarity" in the bibliography is a false friend.** Ref [67] ("Classical-nonclassical polarity of Gaussian states") uses "polarity" for classical-vs-nonclassical complementarity — a binary partition, not the ±sign polarity of a trit.
- **Squeezing↔entanglement conservation is a binary pair, not ternary.** The kinetic/potential-energy analogy (L281–284) converts between exactly *two* nonclassical resources — no third state, no null.
- **Counter to the 2-threshold compute tax.** If one strained to call the Fano off/enhance points a "switch," it is at most a 1-threshold on/off (suppress/enhance), never a 2-threshold ternary gate — and even that is continuous, so the Tau compute-loss thesis does not even apply.

## 5. One-liner

A voltage-tuned, Fano-controlled plasmonic four-wave-mixing frequency comb that continuously dials quantum entanglement for photonic quantum computers — a genuine photonic-engineering result that contains no transistor, no 3-state physics, and no push/pull/null primitive useful to Tau.
