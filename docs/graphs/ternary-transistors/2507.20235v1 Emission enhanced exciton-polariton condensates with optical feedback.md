## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **VERDICT: this is purely photonic and cryogenic, and it is not even a transistor — it is a feedback gain/amplification loop.** There is no electronic ternary path of any kind. It sits one rung *further* from the hunt target than the sibling all-optical polariton transistor (1201.4071): that paper was a binary switch; this paper only amplifies emission intensity, so it is not a compute primitive at all. It neither gives Tau a native 3-state transistor nor flatters it with a native-floor density claim — it is orthogonal to the ternary question.
- **"Transistor operation" appears only in the citations, not the results.** Ref. [20–23] (polariton transistor switch, all-optical polariton transistor) are invoked in the Intro as *motivation*; the present paper's actual deliverable is a 110% intensity gain from re-seeding, i.e. amplification, not switching, not gating, not three-level anything.
- **The central quantity is a continuous scalar, not a trit.** η = (⟨I_F⟩−⟨I⟩)/⟨I⟩ is a real-valued gain ratio; it is never quantized into {−1, 0, +1}. There is no push/pull/null encoding, no one-hot two-bit trit, no sign-bearing state.
- **The "transistor tax" does not even arise — it is one threshold, not two.** The system's only nonlinearity is the condensation threshold P_th ≈ 55 pJ, a single binary on/off knee. A ternary cell needs two distinguishable thresholds; this device has exactly one, so it is a binary discriminator by construction.
- **The one genuinely signed degree of freedom is removed at the model level, not merely disabled.** The theory is explicitly "scalar (no polarization)" (Eq. 2 context) — the polariton spin/pseudospin (±1) that the sibling paper flagged as "advantageous in future devices" is absent from the physics here entirely. Even the speculative σ⁺/σ⁻ → push/pull path is closed in this paper.
- **There is no "pull".** Feedback only *seeds* (adds photons via stimulated scattering); the mechanism is sign-positive injection. Nothing removes population or flips a sign. The −1 trit has no realization.
- **The "blockade" is a suppression, not a negative polarity.** The blueshift U(t) "classical optical blockade" (Fig.5e) *reduces* the gain by detuning the seed out of resonance — it is a loss of enhancement, not a signed "pull" operation. Easy to misread as a polarity signal; it is actually just gain roll-off.
- **The "null" is binary's zero.** Below P_th nothing is emitted; this is the ordinary off state, identical to a binary 0, not a middle null distinguishable from both push and pull.
- **The neuromorphic aspiration is transport/interconnect, not compute.** The "connect multiple condensates … vector–matrix multiplication" proposal (N15/N16) is about *routing weighted signals between nodes* — it touches the transport half of the Tau thesis at best, and only as a photonic analogy. It says nothing about the compute half, the 2-threshold tax, or 3ⁿ addressing.
- **Energy/thermal figures do not transfer.** 4 K cryogenic operation (liquid helium) and per-optical-switch picosecond dynamics are incommensurable with Tau's room-temperature electronic wire-transport numbers (0.05 vs 1.20 pJ per trit). No per-cell area is measured, so no density claim exists to refute or flatter.

## 5. One-liner

A cryogenic photonic feedback loop that re-seeds an exciton-polariton condensate to amplify its emission by up to 110% and proposes recurrently linking condensates for neuromorphic vector–matrix work — a scalar-gain transport/interconnect primitive with no sign, no third state, and not even a switching function, hence no native 3-state transistor for Tau, no electronic ternary path, and nothing that reaches the compute half of the ternary thesis.
