## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **VERDICT: this is a standard FET compact model, NOT a native 3-state transistor — and it fails the hunt *before* reaching the "0.63× native-floor flattery" trap.** It is an equilibrium charge-density model (2DEG/2DHG populations from electrostatics + a single-subband quantum well). It proposes or demonstrates no switchable push/pull/null conduction, and its "transistor" in the title is aspirational: gate/source/drain characteristics are explicitly deferred to "the next stage of this study" (§V).
- **"Polar" is a false-friend, not a polarity encoding.** "Polar" here means *polarization-induced* (spontaneous + piezoelectric) carrier gases. It has zero to do with "polarity encodes the sign of the trit." The name collision flatters the hunt on arrival; come to terms and it evaporates.
- **The electron/hole pair does NOT give independent push/pull.** In an undoped periodic channel, charge neutrality *locks* q·ns = q·ps (Eq.8): the 2DEG is always accompanied by an equal-density 2DHG. The natural states are {off} (below threshold) and {matched electron+hole pair} (above) — never {electron-only} or {hole-only}. There is no independent "push" or "pull" state to address, so no ternary set {−1, 0, +1} is realizable from the intrinsic physics.
- **The hole gas is actively DEPLETED, not exploited.** Section IV's entire purpose is to eliminate the 2DHG via donor δ-/modulation doping because it is "undesirable... parasitic parallel conduction" that "deteriorate[s] device performance." The paper converts a would-be two-carrier structure *back to one carrier* (electron) for performance — the exact opposite of a native-ternary ambition. The third state is thrown away, not harvested.
- **There is no independent "null" between signs.** Because electron and hole densities are equal-and-locked, you cannot isolate a null by "turning off one sign" — you get both or neither. Doping shifts the balance (electron-rich by killing holes) as a fabrication-time static choice, not a runtime logic state. The 2-bit one-hot encoding (01 push / 00 null / 10 pull, mutually exclusive) has no counterpart in a device whose two signs are *conjoined* by electrostatics.
- **The energy cost is band-gap-scale, not a free null.** The null (no carriers, VT < 0) is free, but reaching the "push/pull" pair costs a potential drop exceeding the band gap (~3.44 eV for GaN, Eq.16/17) — a structural cost ≈137×kT, not a logic-level trade-off. This *instantiates* the 2-threshold tax, but at a scale that confirms ternary LOSES on compute here; it is not a "free 3-state."
- **Spontaneous polarization is a static DC bias, not a controllable state.** P is a fixed, always-on material property (metal-polar reference); it produces a permanent built-in carrier population with no gate or doping input. That is a *free ON* (population), the inverse of the Tau "free null costs ~0" thesis, and it is not switchable.
- **The "free null" here is just binary's off.** Below threshold nothing conducts — but that is the off-state every FET already has. A middle null distinct from both push and pull does not exist; the null is only "below threshold."
- **Net:** not the hunt target (no native ternary transistor), and not even the trap (it makes no 3-state or density-ratio claim to debunk). Its one genuinely signed structure — negative electrons / positive holes — is real but is *locked into a matched pair and then half of it is deleted as parasitic*. It contributes nothing to the compute half of the Tau thesis, and nothing to the transport half (no switching, no per-trit energy, no density-ratio figure).

## 5. One-liner

A polarization-induced (spontaneous + piezoelectric) 2DEG/2DHG compact model for III-Nitride multichannel FETs whose electron and hole gases are charge-neutrality-locked into a matched pair and whose hole gas is then actively depleted as parasitic is a standard equilibrium FET charge model — its "polar" is polarization, not polarity — that yields no native, independently-addressable push/pull/null conduction and so offers Tau neither a 3-state transistor nor even a density-ratio claim to debunk, only a band-gap-scale (≈3.44 eV) confirmation of the 2-threshold tax.
