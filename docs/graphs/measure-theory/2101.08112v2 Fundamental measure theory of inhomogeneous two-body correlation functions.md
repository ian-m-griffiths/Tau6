## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **"Measure" means valuation here.** The "fundamental measures" are the intrinsic volumes of the hard sphere — additive, motion-invariant functionals (Hadwiger's theorem). This is the valuation reading of "measure", not the σ-algebra reading; both live in our folder and must not be conflated.
- **FMT route beats PY route by removing the closure**: FMT computes c⁽²⁾ directly from the generating functional (no closure, no self-consistent iteration); PY needs nonlinear iteration. Counter-to "integral-equation closures are the standard way to get pair correlations".
- **Counts, not normalized densities**: FMT uses unnormalized weighted densities (number per volume, with the ln(1−n₃) excluded-volume term); nothing is normalized to sum 1 — the same "absolute math" instinct as ours.
- **Inhomogeneity breaks translational invariance**: in bulk, Fourier diagonalizes everything; inhomogeneous (wall/confined) systems lose that luxury — the paper's entire effort is recovering a diagonal structure (per-k, per-n) via symmetry reduction.
