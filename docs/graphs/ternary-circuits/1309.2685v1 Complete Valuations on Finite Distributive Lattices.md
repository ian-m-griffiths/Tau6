## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **"Complete valuation" ≠ measure-theoretic "complete"** (terminological collision, cf. Westerbaan paper 5 and the MEASURE THEORY lens): here "complete" means the initial/final-segment join/meet closure (Def 2.3), not Π/Σ-completeness of measures. Our project must not read this as "complete measure".
- **Valuation is additive-modular, our Eisenstein norm is multiplicative**: N(a+bω)=a²+ab+b² is NOT a valuation in Marigo's sense (modularity is v(a∨b)+v(a∧b)=v(a)+v(b); N satisfies N(xy)=N(x)N(y)). Mapping N onto this paper's "valuation" would be an over-claim (guardrail: `porting_map.md` D1-style).
- **Bijective valuations need NOT be complete** (Question 2.1 open): there is a genuine gap between "numbering the lattice" and "numbering with join/meet closure" — the harder question is left open, so any claim "our numbering is a complete valuation" must be checked against Def 2.3, not assumed.

A rigorous characterization of "numberings of a lattice that respect joins/meets" (complete valuations ⇔ posets of dimension ≤ 2 with a two-order realizer), which legitimizes our per-trit energy as an additive lattice valuation and poses a testable question on our 7-hex↔balanced-ternary and 7ⁿ addressing bijections.
