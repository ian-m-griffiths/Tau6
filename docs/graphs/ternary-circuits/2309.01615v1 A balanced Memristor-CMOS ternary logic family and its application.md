## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **Their balanced encoding is 3 DC voltage LEVELS (−VDD/0/+VDD) — not our push/pull/null direction encoding.** Their "0" is a driven mid-level (GND), which costs energy; our null is "no line energized" (TernaryCell `null_is_free`, energy 0). Same trit values, opposite physical story: level-based vs polarity/AC-like. This paper is real, working balanced-ternary *logic* — but it is MLT-3/PAM-3-style DC signaling, not the one-wire AC-like scheme of TERNARY_PROCESSOR.md §1.2.
- **Power is measured per-component (ΣV·I), not per-transition line energy.** Their energy comparison is about circuit topology (decoder vs mux), not about the trit's encoding cost — our TernaryCell (avg 2/3 wire per trit) measures something they never count.
- **Balanced ternary exists in silicon.** Their §6 fabricated memristor-CMOS half adder — DIRECT counter-evidence to any claim that balanced-ternary hardware "doesn't exist"; but it also shows ternary did NOT displace binary — consistent with our TERNARY_PROCESSOR.md §1.5.
- **"Multiplication does not generate carry"** is true only for trit×trit (sign product); positional multi-trit multiply still adds partial products — matches our `tmul_trits` (shift-add accumulation) and the carry-rule caution in TERNARY_PROCESSOR.md §2.2.

## 5. One-liner

"A working, measured memristor-CMOS balanced-ternary family whose TMIN/TMAX/STI/half-adder/multiplier truth tables are EXACTLY our `tand`/`tor`/`tneg`/`tadd1`/`tmul` cells — proof the gate set we synthesize in Verilog matches real silicon — while its level-based, driven-zero encoding is the concrete counter-example to (and benchmark for) our null-is-free polarity cell."
