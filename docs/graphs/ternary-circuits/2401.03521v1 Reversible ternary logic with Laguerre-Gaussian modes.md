## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **Only binary AND ternary are maximally symmetric** — the paper's key structural claim: for n≥4 the pairwise distances within the alphabet are no longer equal. Ternary is the densest system whose phase alphabet keeps every pair equally distinguishable. (This is an argument for radix-3's *geometric* optimality in phase space, independent of the classic radix-economy argument.)
- **"Reversible" is a different energy story than ours.** Their energy saving comes from Landauer/reversibility (no information erasure). Our TernaryCell saving comes from encoding density (fewer energized lines, null=free). Both are "ternary lowers energy" but the mechanisms are disjoint.
- **Their trits are UNBALANCED phase states [0, 2π/3, 4π/3]** — not {−1,0,+1}. No negation symmetry, no null; "balanced" plays no role. The paper is ternary-in-name, phase-coded-in-fact.
- **The digit set IS the 3rd roots of unity** — i.e. the Z₃ subgroup of the Z₆ unit group of ℤ[ω] (see §4) — an accidental but real structural match with our gauge group.
