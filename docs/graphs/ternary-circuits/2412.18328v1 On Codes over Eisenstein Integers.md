## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **Density cuts both ways**: Eisenstein = densest packing = most energy-efficient, but ALSO closest-packed = most interference-prone — the paper's entire set-partitioning machinery exists to repair the error margin that density costs. Counter to any naive "hex is strictly better" energy story.
- **Gaussian is NOT the natural 2D lattice for constellations**: Gaussian sizes must be sums of two squares; Eisenstein has no such constraint (N13) — a concrete reversal of the "Gaussian = standard complex lattice" default, and the exact kind of i₋₁-vs-i_ω separation our gauge-variant table (GAUGE_VARIANTS.md §1 A) asserts abstractly.
- **Hexagonal weight ≠ norm**: N-equal ⇒ wtHex-equal, but the converse FAILS (Remark 12: wtHex(4+4ρ)=4=wtHex(3+4ρ), norms 16≠13). Independently confirms our guardrail — "hex norm = ring" is FORBIDDEN (proofs/AGENTS.md guardrail 8; porting_map D1).
- **wtHex is not multiplicative**: only sub-multiplicative (wtHex(αθ) ≤ wtHex(α)wtHex(θ)); and wtHex(α)=wtHex(θ)=n iff α ~ n+kρ (Theorem 11 v) — the weight is a much finer object than the norm.
- **Their ρ is 120° (norm a²+b²−ab) — ours is 60° (norm a²+ab+b²)**. Same ring: the isomorphism φ(a,b)=(a,−b) is PROVED in our ledger (ConventionBridge.lean C1). Not a contradiction — a gauge collision resolved by our own theorem.
