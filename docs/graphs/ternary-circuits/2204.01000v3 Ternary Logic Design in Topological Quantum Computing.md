## 3. Counter-to / reversal edges

- **Ternary is NOT free in quantum: braiding alone is not universal** (N11). Non-Clifford gates need *topological charge measurement* — the ternary advantage in circuit compactness does not translate to gate simplicity in the anyonic model. Reversal of the "ternary = more compact = simpler" intuition.
- **The adder is unbalanced-mod-3, not balanced:** their half-adder truth table computes A+B mod 3 with carry (2+2 → S=1, cout=1), i.e. plain positional ternary, not our balanced symmetric encoding. Same "add 3 digits with bounded carry" *shape*, different digit convention.
- **The 37% claim is about state count, not energy:** n_m = log_m N qudits (fewer *wires*), nothing about energy per transition — the compactness is information-theoretic (log₂3), not physical.
- **"Compact ternary" comes at the cost of garbage outputs and ancilla** (N12) — the gate-count accounting includes constant inputs and discarded garbage qutrits; not a free lunch.
- **Quantum-dimension √3 of metaplectic X is *not* our √3:** tempting, but d_X = √3 is a fusion-space growth rate; our hex-lattice √3's (packing, triangle area) are geometry constants — a numerical coincidence, not a shared theorem.
